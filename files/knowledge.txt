EVIO Charging Rates:
- Normal AC Charging: Rs 15 per kWh.
- Fast DC Charging: Rs 20 per kWh.
- Idle fee: Rs 5 per minute if the car is left connected after charging is 100% complete.

EVIO Membership Plans:
- Free Tier: Pay as you go, no monthly fee.
- Pro Tier: Rs 500 per month. Gives 10% discount on charging rates and zero idle fees for the first 30 minutes.
- Elite Tire: Rs 1000 per month. Gives 15% discount on charging rates and zero idle fees for the first 30 minutes.

Common Troubleshooting:
- If the charger doesn't start, make sure the connector is firmly pushed into the car's port until you hear a click.
- If the payment fails in the app, your charging session will pause. Please add money to your EVIO wallet to resume.
