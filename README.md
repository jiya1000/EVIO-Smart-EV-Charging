# ⚡ EVIO – Smart EV Charging Platform

**Charge Smart. Earn Rewards. Drive Green.**

## 📌 About the Project

EVIO is a smart EV charging platform designed to make electric vehicle charging more convenient, accessible, and user-friendly.

It helps EV owners discover nearby charging stations, book charging slots in advance, compare charging options, and monitor battery health through an integrated platform.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

EV users often face challenges such as:
- Difficulty finding nearby charging stations.
- Long waiting times at charging stations.
- Lack of transparent charging cost information.
- Limited awareness about battery health and charging habits.

EVIO aims to address these challenges through a unified digital solution.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Smart Station Discovery:** Find nearby EV charging stations and service centers through an interactive map.
- **Online Slot Booking:** Book charging slots in advance to reduce waiting time.
- **Flexible Payments:** Monthly subscription plans and pay-per-use options.
- **AC & DC Charging:** Access different charging options according to user requirements.
- **Battery Health Management:** Monitor AC/DC charging patterns and receive battery-care suggestions.
- **Rewards & Cashback:** Earn rewards through charging activities.
- **AI-Powered Chatbot:** RAG-based chatbot to assist users with EV-related queries.
- **Digital Advertising:** Advertising opportunities through digital screens at charging stations.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Users explore nearby charging stations.
2. They select a suitable station and charging type.
3. Users book an available charging slot.
4. The platform provides charging-related information and battery health insights.
5. Users can earn rewards and access additional services.
